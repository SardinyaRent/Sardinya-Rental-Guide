---
name: Sardinya customer guide scope
description: Standing product rules for the Sardinya customer-facing rental guide.
---

The Sardinya site is an information guide only: do not add booking flows or links to the official Sardinya website. Put Sassari first in every office list and label it as the central and administrative office (Italian: “Sede centrale e Sede amministrativa”). Show the central contact email first and in red for communications, complaints, and refund requests. Keep the interface available in Italian, English, Spanish, French, German, and Dutch.

The user-provided Sardinya wordmark is the company's logo and the approved brand mark for the site.

**Why:** the user set these product constraints.

**How to apply:** keep these rules consistent across all pages, office lists, contact details, translations, and the shared site header. Do not replace the wordmark with a text-only mark unless asked.
